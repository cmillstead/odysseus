Package the Model/LLM route domain into **one co-located `routes/model/` package** — `routes.py` (model),
`assistant.py`, `copilot.py` — behind three `sys.modules` alias shims, behavior-preserving, as one PR.

one test — fixing it correctly is the headline risk that warms the pattern before Cookbook (02-03).

`app.py` repointed (3 lines); the shared import-state helper + 4 reload-site tests fixed for the shim; 2 source-path
