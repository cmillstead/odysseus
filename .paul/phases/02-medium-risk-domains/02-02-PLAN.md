one test — fixing it correctly is the headline risk that warms the pattern before Cookbook (02-03).

(3 lines); copilot's intra-domain import repointed to the canonical path; the shared import-state helper fixed for
the shim; 2 source-path tests repointed; a guard test. A PR to `dev`.
