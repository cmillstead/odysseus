<objective>
## Goal
Package the Cookbook route domain into **one co-located `routes/cookbook/` package** — `routes.py`
(cookbook routes), `helpers.py`, `output.py` — behind three `sys.modules` alias shims,
behavior-preserving, as one PR. Slice 2 domain 8 of 9 (third/last Phase-2 / MEDIUM domain).

## Purpose
Advances v0.1 (completes Phase 2). Largest domain by line count (4,941 ln) but mechanically the
SIMPLEST Phase-2 move: unlike 02-02, the 02-01 shim-reload gotcha is ABSENT (no cookbook test evicts/
reloads the modules — verified in GROUND three ways), so there is NO shared-test-helper change and NO
reload-site edits. It is a clean 3-file move behind 3 shims with a genuine internal DAG
(`routes → {helpers, output}`) that resolves cleanly through the shims.

## Output
`routes/cookbook/` package (3 byte-identical submodules + minimal `__init__`); three shims at the old
flat paths; `app.py` repointed (1 line); 9 source-path literals repointed across 4 test files; a guard
test. A PR to `dev`.
